### Internship
- **Hithink RoyalFlush Cloud Software Co., Ltd.**
- **AIGC Algorithm Intern** 
- Developed an end-to-end GAN-based lip-sync generation framework to address the long inference times and high GPU memory consumption of diffusion-based models. The model was trained on data generated by large video-generation models and large-scale real-world datasets. In the first stage, the lip-sync model learned frontal-view lip synchronization and facial reconstruction. In the second stage, multiple classifiers were used to identify challenging inputs, including extreme profile angles, infants, and occlusions, followed by targeted fine-tuning on specialized datasets. To mitigate unstable GAN training and mode collapse, I designed a uniform sampling strategy for extreme angles during profile-model training, improving profile-view generation quality without compromising the model's original performance.
